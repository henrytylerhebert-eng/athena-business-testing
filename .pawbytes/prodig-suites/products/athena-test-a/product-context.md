# Product context: Athena Test A

## Problem

First-semester transfer students with 30–60 accepted credits and unresolved applicability questions must synthesize fragmented advising information to identify the correct next action.

## Vision

Test a human-reviewed decision-clarity layer that shows position, next three moves, blockers, and an advisor question without replacing institutional systems or professional judgment.

## Users

- Primary: eligible transfer-student tester
- Operational: facilitator/researcher
- Review: authorized advisor or staff reviewer

## Success definition

The test produces inspectable per-tester evidence about clarity, correctness, time, system switching, confidence, corrections, guardrails, and verified seven-day action completion.

## Constraints

- Synthetic, local-only prototype
- Pseudonymous records
- No production integrations
- No automated academic decisions
- No fabricated go/no-go threshold
