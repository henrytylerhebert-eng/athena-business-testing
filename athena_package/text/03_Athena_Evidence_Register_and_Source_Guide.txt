Athena Evidence Register and Source Guide

Traceability rules for the research package

Source documents included

Canonical source | Primary role | Notes

Know Your Next Move: Reconstructing the Student Advising Problem and Designing the Smallest Test | Broad problem reconstruction, segment comparison, competitive framing, MVP and first experiment | Contains citations to institutional, government, financial-aid, NCAA, and advising sources.

UL Lafayette Student Advising Validation Study and Read-Only Student Path Agent | Institution-specific segment, data, architecture, governance, and experiment validation | The two uploaded copies are byte-for-byte identical; one canonical copy is retained.

Institutional Intelligence Assessment: University of Louisiana at Lafayette | Leadership, strategy, financial, systems, advising, reporting, stakeholder, and opportunity context | Contains a final evidence register header, but several sections still require claim-level verification before external publication.

Evidence labels

Label | Use

Verified / sourced | Directly supported by a named source that can be opened and inspected.

Institution-reported | Describes the institution’s stated process, metric, or system.

Peer-reviewed evidence | Supported by academic research; applicability to Athena remains a product inference.

Inference | A strategic interpretation derived from multiple facts or patterns.

Unverified | A claim, workflow detail, estimate, or recommendation that still needs direct confirmation.

Claims that should not be treated as settled

Exact demand from students, recruiters, advisors, community colleges, or UL Lafayette buyers.

Exact Banner table/API availability and institutional willingness to provide extracts.

Exact transfer-credit evaluation latency for the intended pilot cohort.

Advisor time savings, student action rates, retention impact, and tuition preservation.

Procurement pathway, budget ownership, and willingness to license at the department level.

Whether the recruiter or community-college use cases preserve enough of Athena’s core product advantage.

Recommended source-audit format

Claim | Source URL | Source type | Exact passage/page | Status | Where used | Notes

Example: Transfer students lose a significant share of credits | Add authoritative URL | Government / research | Page or section | Verify | Segment rationale | Do not rely on an unsourced summary statistic.
