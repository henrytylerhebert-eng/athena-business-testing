## Status definitions

- **Backlog:** proposed; no participant evidence captured.
- **Designed:** protocol and decision gates are documented.
- **Running:** evidence collection is active.
- **Complete:** raw evidence, synthesis, and a resulting decision are linked.
- **Stopped:** the stop criterion was reached or the test was invalidated.
