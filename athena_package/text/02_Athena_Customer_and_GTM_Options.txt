Athena Customer and Go-to-Market Options

Digestible choices for founder discussion

These options are not mutually exclusive forever. The decision is which learning loop to run first.

Option | First user | Value proposition | Why consider it | Primary risk | Current view

A. UL Lafayette transfer advising pilot | First-semester transfer students, 30–60 credits, unresolved equivalencies | Student clarity + advisor preparation + institutional retention value | Deepest research base; measurable within a registration window | Institutional access, governance, data latency, slow sponsorship | Highest evidence / moderate speed

B. Community-college pathway bridge | Prospective or transferring community-college students | Understand transferability, applicability, next steps, and destination options | Clear transition pain; potentially strong regional ecosystem fit | Requires partner alignment and articulation accuracy | Promising / under-researched

C. University recruiter field tool | Prospective students interacting with recruiters | Collect lightweight student context and return a useful, institution-informed next-step plan | Fast desirability testing; low integration; recruiter-led distribution | May drift toward enrollment CRM or lead capture rather than student agency | Fastest test / least validated

D. Direct-to-student decision-clarity prototype | Students with a fictional or self-entered scenario | Position + next moves + blockers without institutional integration | Tests the core UX cleanly and cheaply | Weak evidence of willingness to pay and institutional workflow fit | Best UX test / weak GTM proof

Recommended sequencing

Run a lightweight, non-integrated desirability test across Options B and C to learn language, urgency, and workflow demand.

In parallel, retain Option A as the strongest decision-grade institutional pilot hypothesis because it has the clearest evidence chain and measurable outcomes.

Use Option D as the common prototype layer so the same core interface can be tested across contexts.

Do not build live SIS integrations until the simplified experience demonstrates behavioral value and an institutional sponsor confirms the workflow.

Decision criteria

Criterion | Question

Founder fit | Which customer and problem most clearly express Grant’s long-term product thesis?

Learning speed | Which test can run with the fewest dependencies?

Evidence quality | Will the result change a product or GTM decision?

Measurable value | Can the test connect to a concrete action, time savings, enrollment, retention, or credit applicability outcome?

Expansion path | Does the initial workflow create a credible path to broader institutional value?
