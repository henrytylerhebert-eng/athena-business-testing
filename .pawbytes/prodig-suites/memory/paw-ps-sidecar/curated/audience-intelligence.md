# Curated audience intelligence: Athena Test A

## Transfer-student tester

- Arrives with prior higher-education experience
- Must distinguish accepted credits from credits that apply to the selected major
- Approaches a time-sensitive registration decision
- Must combine information across degree audit, transfer evaluation, portal, and advisor communication
- Needs plain-language priority and a clear path to human help

## Facilitator

- Needs a repeatable protocol and pseudonymous record
- Must distinguish automatic observation, participant self-report, and reviewer judgment
- Needs per-tester drill-down, aggregate signals, exports, and visible unknowns
- Must not use the prototype as an institutional system of record

## Advisor reviewer

- Verifies action correctness
- Corrects or routes exceptions
- Retains authority over substitutions, overrides, applicability, and hold removal

Direct audience interviews and observed Athena demand are not included in the research package.
