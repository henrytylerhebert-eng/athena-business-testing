# Evidence handling

Athena uses four evidence classes:

| Class | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Sourced** | Directly supported by a named, inspectable source |
| **Institution-reported** | Describes a process, system, or metric stated by the institution |
| **Inference** | Strategic interpretation derived from facts or patterns |
| **Unverified** | Claim or hypothesis still requiring direct confirmation |

## Test evidence layers

Keep these layers separate:

1. **Raw observation** — what happened or was said.
2. **Structured finding** — repeated or decision-relevant pattern.
3. **Interpretation** — what the team believes the finding means.
4. **Decision** — what changes because of the evidence.

A polished portal, participant preference, synthetic scenario, or queued institutional conversation is not proof of demand or business readiness.

## Minimum evidence record

Every completed test should retain:

- protocol and participant definition;
- consent and privacy handling where applicable;
- raw or structured observations;
- exclusions, failures, and protocol deviations;
- evidence classification;
- resulting decision and revisit condition.
