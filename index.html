01 · Current readout

What the research says now

The work has narrowed the thesis without closing the founder’s decision. The problem is supported; the buyer and first route remain open.

01

Problem supported

Students face fragmented systems, cognitive overload, unclear next actions, and blockers discovered too late.

02

Product shape narrowed

“My Position + My Next Three Moves + My Blockers” is the smallest useful experience identified for testing.

03

Buyer still open

Department, student-success, recruiter, community-college, and direct-to-student entry points still require demand evidence.

Strongest current hypothesis
Test whether a radically simplified view of position, next actions, deadlines, and blockers improves decision clarity and action completion.

This is a learning hypothesis, not a final product verdict. The first test should avoid production SIS integration and automated academic decisions.

Most evidence-supported institutional test First-semester UL Lafayette transfer students with 30–60 accepted credits and unresolved applicability questions.

Evidence boundary: No direct demand, buyer commitment, willingness-to-pay, advisor time savings, retention impact, or production integration feasibility has been verified. Those remain explicit learning targets.

02 · Decision trail

How each question changed the work

A traceable path from a broad empowerment idea to a narrow behavioral test—then back to the unresolved customer question.

1
Starting thesisStudent empowerment

Question created: What exact student problem is painful, frequent, measurable, and small enough to test?

2
Problem reframedDecision clarity

Students already have information. The gap is usable synthesis and prioritization. Question: who feels the strongest pain and urgency?

3
Segment comparedTransfer students

Credit applicability and registration consequences made transfer students the strongest general segment. Question: can it be narrowed at one institution?

4
Context narrowedUL Lafayette

The proposed cohort became first-semester transfers with 30–60 credits and unresolved equivalencies. Question: what is the smallest data footprint?

5
Experience reducedThree-panel view

Position, next three moves, and blockers—with advisor-verified actions and no automated academic decisions. Question: does it change behavior?

6
Proof definedBehavioral pilot

Measure whether students identify and complete the right next action with fewer corrections and less system switching. Question: which entry point first?

7
Decision reopenedCustomer route

Community colleges and recruiters may offer faster entry, but create different users and value propositions. Decision: which route preserves the thesis and learns fastest?

04 · Smallest test

Prove clarity before infrastructure

The package converges on a shared prototype layer that can travel across customer contexts while keeping academic judgment human-owned.

Design constraints

01
Decision layer, not system of record

Translate and prioritize approved information; do not replace Banner, DegreeWorks, or advisors.

02
Three panels, no dashboard sprawl

Current position, next three moves, and active blockers are the minimum useful surface.

03
Advisor-verified actions

No automated academic decision, course substitution, or degree-applicability ruling.

04
Manual data is acceptable first

Concierge assembly or secure extracts can test behavior without production integration.

05
Behavior over preference

Measure correct action identification, completion, correction, confidence, and system switching.
