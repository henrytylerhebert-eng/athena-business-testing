Athena Customer Discovery Workspace

A guided research package for Grant Dozier

Purpose:  This package is designed to help Grant inspect the reasoning, challenge assumptions, compare customer options, and decide what to test next. It is not a final verdict on his product.

How to use this package

5-minute path: Read the Current Readout and the Customer Options table.

30-minute path: Follow the Research Journey from original thesis to current hypotheses.

Deep-review path: Open the source library and evidence register to inspect the underlying material.

Working-session path: Use the decision prompts at the end to compare options with Grant in real time.

Current readout

The strongest current hypothesis is not “build a comprehensive advising platform.” It is to test whether a radically simplified, student-centered view of position, next actions, deadlines, and blockers improves decision clarity and action completion.

[Inference] The most evidence-supported first institutional test is a narrowly defined transfer-student advising use case at UL Lafayette, especially first-semester transfer students with 30–60 accepted credits and unresolved transfer-credit applicability questions.

[Unverified] A lighter-weight recruiter or community-college pathway experience may offer a faster path to desirability testing, but that option has not been validated to the same depth as the UL Lafayette advising use case.

What is supported vs. what is still open

Category | Current status | Meaning

Problem | Supported | Students face fragmented systems, cognitive overload, unclear next actions, and reactive blockers.

Initial segment | Supported with narrowing | Transfer students show high pain; UL Lafayette work narrows the cohort to 30–60 credits with unresolved equivalencies.

Product shape | Supported as a test hypothesis | Position + Next Three Moves + Blockers is the smallest useful experience identified.

Institutional integration | Partially supported | A concierge or secure-extract pilot appears feasible; live API integration is unnecessary for the first test.

Buyer/GTM | Open | Departmental, student-success, recruiter, and community-college entry points remain options to compare.

Demand | Unverified | Direct student, recruiter, advisor, and institutional interviews are still required.
