# Curated market and route intelligence: Athena Test A

Route A has the deepest current research base and the clearest proposed behavioral outcomes. Its principal disadvantages are institutional access, governance, data latency, and sponsor speed.

Existing systems already hold authoritative records and support degree auditing and registration. Athena's proposed differentiation is translation and prioritization, not replacement of Banner, DegreeWorks, ULink, TES, advisors, or institutional decision processes.

Buyer demand, procurement path, budget ownership, willingness to pay, retention value, and advisor time savings remain unverified. No market-size measurement is used in this brief.
