# Athena output standards

- Preserve the evidence classes Sourced, Institution-reported, Inference, and Unverified.
- Show `No measurements found` when no participant records exist.
- Show `Unknown` when a field, threshold, approval, or outcome is not established.
- Never seed demonstration records into measured dashboards.
- Separate automatic observations, self-report, facilitator judgment, and follow-up evidence.
- Do not collect direct identifiers or sensitive student data in the static prototype.
- Do not describe synthetic completion as live institutional evidence.
